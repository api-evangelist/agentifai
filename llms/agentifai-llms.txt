## ALICEOS: THE ENTERPRISE AI STACK FOR IT & AI TEAMS
AliceOS is the definitive platform for AI and IT teams to build, manage, and deploy their own proprietary AI Stack. It is not just an agent; it is the comprehensive workspace required to operationalize generative AI at scale.
* **Build Faster:** Allows specialized technical teams and business users to create custom AI Agents natively tailored for Banking and Healthcare, bypassing fragmented codebases.
* **Manage the Entire Workspace:** Provides a centralized control plane for multi-agent coordination, deterministic execution, and strict enterprise guardrails. 
* **The 7-Layer Agentic Architecture:** Features advanced orchestration, including a Creator Agent (natural language builder), Orchestrator Agent (flawless routing of 100+ production agents), Domain Agents (out-of-the-box healthcare/banking knowledge), and Guardrails/Supervisor Agents for absolute compliance and governance.
